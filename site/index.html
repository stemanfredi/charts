    Not for navigation. Positions, depths and coastlines come from open datasets of varying accuracy; depths are a coarse elevation model, not soundings.

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), © OpenMapTiles, served by OpenFreeMap; depths GEBCO Compilation Group (2026) GEBCO 2026 Grid (doi:10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa); land elevation Mapzen Terrain Tiles; ocean data generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information. Each layer's source, licence and resolution are behind its ⓘ in Layers.

    Tile requests tell each provider which area you are viewing; a hidden layer makes none. A line's depth profile reads the depth tiles along that line. Your location, when you show it, stays in this page.

    Drawings stay in this browser: saved here as you go, never sent anywhere. Download › Drawing (GeoJSON), in the bar, writes it as a file, with the view, to share or keep it; links do not carry drawings. Labels are written with the map's own fonts, fetched in blocks of characters, never as text.
