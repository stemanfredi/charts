    Not for navigation. Positions, depths and coastlines come from open datasets of varying accuracy; depths are a coarse elevation model, not soundings.

    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), © OpenMapTiles, served by OpenFreeMap; depths GEBCO Compilation Group (2026) GEBCO 2026 Grid (doi:10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa), and Mapzen Terrain Tiles where it cannot be read; ocean data generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information. Each layer's source, licence and resolution are behind its ⓘ in the Layers panel.

    Tile requests tell each provider which area you are viewing; a hidden layer makes none. A line's depth profile reads the depth tiles along that line.

    Drawings stay in this browser: saved here as you go, never sent anywhere. Export › Drawing (GeoJSON) writes one as a file, with the view, to share or keep it; links do not carry drawings. Labels are written with the map's own fonts, fetched in blocks of characters, never as text.
